📊 HR Analytics Dashboard – Power BI
📌 Project Overview

The HR Analytics Dashboard is an interactive Power BI project designed to analyze employee data and identify key workforce trends, employee attrition patterns, and demographic insights.

This dashboard helps HR professionals and business stakeholders make data-driven decisions by providing a clear overview of employee distribution, attrition rates, job satisfaction, salary groups, and work experience.

🎯 Project Objectives
Analyze employee attrition across different departments.
Track key HR performance indicators (KPIs).
Understand employee demographics based on age and gender.
Evaluate employee distribution by salary slab and experience.
Identify potential factors contributing to employee attrition.
Support better workforce planning and employee retention strategies.

📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
Total Employees: 1,417
Active Employees: 1,186
Attrition Count: 231
Attrition Rate: 16.3%
Average Employee Age: 36.94 years
Average Experience: 7.04 years

📊 Dashboard Features
Department-wise Attrition Analysis
Attrition by Salary Slab
Job Role and Job Satisfaction Analysis
Department-wise Employee Count
Age Group Distribution
Gender-wise Attrition Analysis
Attrition Trend by Years of Experience
Interactive Department and Age Group Filters

💡 Key Business Insights
The organization has a total workforce of 1,417 employees.
The overall employee attrition rate is 16.3%.
The Operations department has the highest employee count.
Employees aged 26–35 represent the largest workforce segment.
Higher attrition is observed in the 6–10 LPA salary slab.
Job satisfaction varies across different job roles, highlighting opportunities to improve employee engagement and retention.
